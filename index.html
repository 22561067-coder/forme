A FEW SMALL DETAILS

Nothing hidden
Just good questions

What is FORME?+

A small playground for form and motion. Choose a poster to make its colours and words your own.

Really, no photographs?+

Not a single one. Every graphic is made from shapes, lines, gradients and type.

What is it built with?+

Just HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript. No frameworks, no extra libraries. Simply a browser and a little curiosity.
